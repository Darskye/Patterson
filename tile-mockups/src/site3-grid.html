05Process

Four stages,
no surprises

A typical hall bath runs eleven working days. You get the schedule in writing before we start.

Stage 01 — Day 0

Walk & measure

We come to the house, check the substrate and tell you plainly what it needs. Written, itemised estimate inside two days.

Stage 02 — Days 1–3

Prep & dry fit

Substrate flattened, waterproofing applied and flood-tested, the field snapped and dry-laid so you see every cut before it is permanent.

Stage 03 — Days 4–9

Set to the laser

Back-buttered, levelled and clipped. Same crew on day one and day eleven. Site swept and water shut off every evening.

Stage 04 — Days 10–11

Grout & hand over

Joints packed and struck clean, stone sealed, silicone at every change of plane — then we walk it with you under a work light.

06Spec sheet

What "done
properly" means

The standards every DTI job is held to — the same on a powder room as on a master suite.

ItemWhat we do Standard
Substrate flatness Self-levelled or floated before any tile is opened. ±3 mm / 3 m
Lippage Levelling clips on every large-format joint, pulled next day. ±1 mm
Mortar coverage Back-buttered and periodically lifted to verify contact. 95% wet areas
Waterproofing Bonded membrane, corners banded, then flood-tested in front of you. 24 hr hold
Movement joints Silicone at every change of plane and at the perimeter. Per TCNA EJ171
Grout joints Packed full, struck clean, consistent from the door to the far corner. ±0.5 mm
Hand-over Walked with you under a raking work light. Anything you find, we fix. Same week

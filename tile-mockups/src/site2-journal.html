01The standardAtlanta, GA
On flatness A finished tile plane is judged in raking light, not overhead light. We check ours with a work lamp held flat against the wall before we grout — because that is how the room will be seen at seven in the morning.

A tile floor is a plane. Either it is true, or it is not — and no amount of grout, sealer or good intention will argue with a straightedge. That single fact is the whole of our trade, and the reason we work the way we do.

Every job begins the same way. We flatten the substrate before we think about the tile. We dry-lay the field so the cuts land where a person's eye will forgive them. We snap lines that answer to the room rather than to a wall that was never square in 1974. It is slower, and it is the only method we have ever been willing to put our name against.

What follows is a selection of recent rooms, the materials we keep coming back to, and how a job with us actually runs from the first walk-through to the last bead of silicone.

04ServicesResidential · Atlanta Metro
01 Bathrooms & wet roomsFull remodels +

Sloped and waterproofed pans, bonded membranes that we flood-test in front of you, niches framed square to the tile rather than to the stud, and stone that carries its veining across every plane it meets. Curbless entries, linear drains, steam-rated assemblies.

02 Kitchens & backsplashesSlab & handmade +

Full-height slab backsplashes and bookmatched range walls cut on site and dry-fitted before a drop of mortar. Handmade zellige and glazed brick, where the whole charm is the irregularity — set so it reads as intentional.

03 Natural stoneMarble, travertine, slate +

Marble, travertine, limestone and slate — honed, sealed and set by hands that know how each one moves. Medallion inlay, decorative borders and diamond-set fields, cut on a wet saw and eased by hand.

04 Floors & large formatUp to 5 ft +

Self-levelled substrates, uncoupling membranes and heated floor systems under large-format porcelain — flat planes with joints you have to look for. Lippage held inside a millimetre across the whole field.

05 Hearths & feature wallsDry-stacked +

Dry-stacked ledger panels, full-height surrounds and stacked stone bars — mechanically supported, mitred at the returns, with no raw edge left showing anywhere a hand can reach.

06 Outdoor & pool decksFreeze-rated +

Travertine pavers, bluestone terraces, coping and pool surrounds set on proper exterior systems — built to take a Georgia August and a January freeze without letting go.

05How a job runsFour stages
01

Walk & measure

We come to the house, look at the substrate and tell you plainly what it needs. Written, itemised estimate inside two days.

02

Layout & dry fit

Substrate flattened, waterproofing applied and flood-tested, the field snapped and dry-laid so you can see every cut before it is permanent.

03

Set

Back-buttered, levelled, clipped. The same crew on day one and day eleven. Site swept and water shut off every evening.

04

Grout & hand over

Joints packed and struck clean, stone sealed, silicone at every change of plane — then we walk it with you under a work light.
